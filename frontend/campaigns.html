How a campaign moves

From a brief to completed work.

Define the work

Create the campaign, connect the client and creators involved, and record the deliverables and outcome the work is expected to produce.

Assign the work

Turn deliverables into tasks with owners, due dates, and enough context for someone to act without searching through old messages.

Review and approve

Keep submissions, revisions, and approvals attached to the campaign so its current state remains visible to the people who need it.

Close the record

When the work is complete, its tasks, activity, and relevant revenue remain connected instead of becoming a separate reconstruction later.

What the desk shows

One campaign. One operational state.

Different people need different views of the same work. Operators need progress and ownership. Creators need their assignments. Clients need the work they are involved in. Internal information stays internal.

Campaign anatomy

The record should answer the important questions.

01 · Brief

What are we doing?

Capture the objective, deliverables, expectations, and definition of done before work starts.

02 · People

Who is involved?

Connect the campaign to its client, creators, and internal owners without duplicating their records.

03 · Activity

What changed?

Keep tasks, approvals, status changes, and relevant financial activity traceable to the campaign.

Visibility by role

The same work does not require the same access.

A campaign can be shared without exposing the agency's entire operation. Visibility should follow responsibility.

Agency

Run the campaign

Manage assignments, deadlines, approvals, internal tasks, and the campaign's wider operational context.

Creator

Do the work

See the assignments, deliverables, deadlines, and feedback relevant to their role in the campaign.

Client

Review the work

Access the briefs, deliverables, files, and approvals that belong to their campaigns without seeing internal agency information.

A campaign is more than a status

Keep the work connected from start to finish.

The useful record is not simply “active” or “complete.” It connects the decisions, people, tasks, approvals, and financial events that explain how the campaign got there.

Work

Each stage should leave enough context behind for the next person to understand what happened and what needs to happen next.

Records

The revenue flow

From the original transaction to the final payout.

Aries records the financial events already produced by your connected platforms. It gives operators one place to understand what was received, what was deducted, how the agreement applies, and what still needs attention.

Revenue is posted

A sale, tip, subscription, or campaign payment enters the ledger as gross revenue, together with its source and related creator or campaign.

Fees are recorded

Platform, processing, or other applicable deductions are recorded against the original transaction so the resulting net amount is clear.

The agreement is applied

The recorded agency and creator split is applied to the relevant amount, making each party's expected share visible before payout.

Payout is tracked

A payout can move through ready, held, cleared, or review states. The ledger keeps the reason visible when money cannot move forward.

Audit trail

The ledger remembers what happened.

Revenue changes should leave a trace. Each event records what happened, when it happened, and whether a connected delivery succeeded or needs another attempt.

The creator profile

Everything the desk needs. Nothing it does not.

A creator profile brings the operational pieces together: identity, active work, financial status, assigned people, and relevant history. Access should follow the user's role, not curiosity.

The working record

Open a creator and understand the situation quickly.

The profile should answer the questions an operator has before taking action: who is this, what are they working on, what needs attention, and what is the current financial state?

Today

What needs attention?

Surface overdue tasks, pending approvals, missing onboarding steps, and other items that require an operator.

In flight

What are they working on?

Connect active campaigns and assignments directly to the creator so current work can be understood without another search.

Financial

What has been paid?

Keep relevant revenue and payout information aligned with the financial ledger rather than maintaining a separate creator spreadsheet.

Data boundaries

A creator profile is an operational record, not a dossier.

Store the information required to operate the relationship and protect everything else. Sensitive fields should have a clear purpose, appropriate access controls, and a defined retention policy.

Collect with a purpose

Information should exist because the workspace needs it for a defined operational, contractual, financial, or administrative purpose.

Restrict by role

Not every team member needs access to every creator field. Permissions should determine what each role can view or change.

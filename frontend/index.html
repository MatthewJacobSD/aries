How Aries fits together

The work stays connected from onboarding to payout.

Aries is not another tool your team has to remember to update. The platform is built around the relationships between the work your agency already does.

Build the relationship

Create the creator or client record, establish the people involved, and give the relationship a clear operational home.

Create the work

Turn a brief into a campaign with deliverables, owners, tasks, deadlines, and the context people need to move it forward.

Move the work forward

Track assignments, submissions, feedback, approvals, blockers, and changes without separating the activity from the campaign.

Understand the result

Connect completed work with relevant revenue and activity so the team can understand what happened, what remains outstanding, and what needs attention next.

Trust by design

Good operations make the state of the business understandable.

Trust is not created by putting more information on a screen. It comes from clear ownership, appropriate access, consistent records, and a useful history of what changed.

Visibility

See the current state

Know which campaigns are moving, which tasks need attention, which creators are active, and which financial records require review.

Accountability

Know who owns the work

Assign responsibility directly to the work. When ownership changes, the history remains with the record rather than disappearing into someone's inbox.

Access

Share without overexposing

Clients, creators, operators, and external partners do not need access to the entire business. Keep visibility aligned with the work and responsibility of each role.

History

Understand what changed

Important activity can remain associated with the relevant record, giving the team a starting point when a status, number, or responsibility changes.

Reconciliation

Know what the numbers mean

Financial activity should remain traceable to its source and status, making outstanding amounts and exceptions easier to review.

Built around your relationships

Everyone involved gets the context they need.

Aries separates collaboration from unrestricted access. Bring the right people into the right workspace and keep the wider operation protected.

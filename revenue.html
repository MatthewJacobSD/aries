How cash moves

From intake to the people who earned it.

Aries does not invent a new bank. It records what already happened on the rails you use, then shows the split in language finance and operators can share.

Intake lands

A sale, tip, or campaign payment posts as gross. The source platform and the creator stay on the row.

Fees come off

Platform and processing fees are withheld on the same event. Net is what remains after those lines, not a guess.

Share is applied

Agency and creator splits follow the agreement on file. A 70/30 is visible before anyone hits pay.

Payout leaves

Ready, held, or cleared. If money is still sitting, the row says why — review, missing details, or a failed rail.

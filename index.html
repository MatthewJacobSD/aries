How the work actually moves

From onboarding to payout, keep the chain connected.

Aries does not ask your team to replace every tool they already use. It gives the operational work around those tools a single place to live.

Bring the creator in

Start with a creator profile, collect the information your team needs, assign the right people, and keep onboarding tasks visible until they are complete.

Turn campaigns into work

Create a campaign, define deliverables, assign tasks, and track approvals. Everyone involved can see what has been requested, submitted, approved, or delayed.

Keep conversations with the work

Client and creator conversations stay next to the campaign, task, or request they relate to. A handoff does not require someone to explain six weeks of messages again.

Reconcile the money

Revenue events, fees, shares, adjustments, and payouts can be reviewed against their source. Finance gets a clearer picture of what is owed, what is pending, and what has already settled.

Why teams stay

Trust comes from knowing what happened.

Growing agencies rarely lose information because nobody is working. They lose it because work is spread across personal inboxes, spreadsheets, chat apps, and disconnected dashboards. Aries gives important operational activity a shared home.

Visibility

See the current state

Campaign status, creator activity, outstanding tasks, and revenue figures are visible to the people responsible for them. Fewer status meetings. Fewer "where are we with this?" messages.

Accountability

Know who handled it

Important changes can be associated with the team member who made them. Ownership becomes part of the workflow instead of something reconstructed from chat history.

Control

Keep sensitive work contained

Workspaces, roles, and permissions help separate internal operations from external collaboration. Access can be reviewed as teams and client relationships change.

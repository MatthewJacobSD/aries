Campaigns

A campaign is the unit of work: brief, deliverables, who is shooting, what is approved, and whether money has posted against it. Status is on the file — not in a thread from last Tuesday.

How a campaign moves

From the brief to a line on the book.

Name the work

Create the campaign, attach creators and a client room, and write what “done” means.

Assign tasks

Each asset or checkpoint has an owner and a due date. Waiting work is visible in the morning.

Approve in place

Submitted, revised, approved. The event can fire a hook without a separate status email.

Tie it to cash

When the period closes, revenue against that campaign is a row, not a reconstruction.

What the desk shows

Everyone sees the same state.

Operators see the queue. Creators see their assignments. Clients see the brief and the approvals — not another client’s campaign.

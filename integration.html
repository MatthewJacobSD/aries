How a hook reads

JSON on the wire. A log on the desk.

Systems still exchange a signed payload. People read one compact line — time, source, status, message.

16:02:11AUDITcampaign.content.approved atlas-september creator_4821 assets 3 by user_0192
16:02:11DELIVER200hook campaign.content.approved → project-bus 36ms
16:07:03DELIVER200hook revenue.payout.ready → treasury-bus 29ms
16:22:51DELIVERRETRYhook client.brief.updated → pager attempt 2
revenue.*

Money events

Posted, adjusted, refunded, ready to pay, cleared. Always named to a creator or campaign.

campaign.*

Work events

Assigned, submitted, approved, revised, done. Enough for a board. Not a dump of the file.

client.* / team.*

Room events

Brief updated, member removed, conversation needs a person. The pager only fires when a rule says so.

What does not leave

A hook is signed. A retry is visible. A secret stays put.

Failed delivery stays on the log until it succeeds. You can show a partner the line without handing them the whole workspace.

Signed payloads

Each event carries a signature. Downstream tools can reject a body that does not match. Demo pages never show the live key.

Delivery you can audit

200, retry, or held. If the pager missed twice, that is a row — not a rumour in chat.
